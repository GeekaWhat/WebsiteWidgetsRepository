DM88 Micro-ATX Budget 1440p Gaming Build

★★★★★

Test build

Reviewed by GeekaWhat Team · Test run: DM88

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Compact Micro-ATX footprint
  • Great value-to-performance focus
  • Strong 1080p and light 1440p results

Cons

  • 16GB RAM is entry-level for newer titles
  • No modular PSU cable management
  • GPU choice is best for 1080p-first targets

Bottom Line

This DM88 build pairs a Ryzen 5 7600X with a Sapphire Pulse RX 9060 XT 8GB in a compact Micro-ATX case. The result is a budget-conscious system that handles 1080p very well and can push into 1440p in many games while keeping overall part cost sensible.
