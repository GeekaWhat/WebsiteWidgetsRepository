MSI MAG B850 TOMAHAWK MAX WiFi

★★★★☆

4.3/5

Reviewed by Jay Harris · February 7, 2025

Pros

  • Fast networking selection
  • Sophisticated aesthetic
  • Competitive pricing

Cons

  • Weak audio options
  • Not fully tool-less
  • No RGB lighting

Bottom Line

The MSI MAG B850 TOMAHAWK MAX WiFi is a competitively priced motherboard with great features. It offers solid expansion options and bountiful connectivity, including 5 Gigabit LAN and WiFi 7, along with a sleek black aesthetic ideal for darker-themed builds. The only real qualm is its lack of tool-less features - adding a few more would greatly improve the user experience and further strengthen its value proposition.
